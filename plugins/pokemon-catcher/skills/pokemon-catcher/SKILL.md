# Pokemon Catcher

After personally verifying a successful commit, closed pull request, or completed ticket/story, call `pokemon_record_milestone` exactly once with the real source, reference, and title.

Never claim rewards for planned, attempted, or partial work. Branches and VesnALIA worktrees in known project repositories are detected automatically; do not manually record a branch reward that the detector will discover.

Use `alia pokemon collection --json` to inspect the collection, `alia pokemon companion <capture-id>` to select any caught or hatched Pokémon as the active companion, and `alia pokemon --help` for command details. Companions retain separate experience. Evolved forms become their own Pokédex entries; item, special, and trade evolution abilities unlock through token milestones.
