Speak with VesnALIA while your agents work across projects. Ask for a briefing, focus a thread, read its latest report, queue an instruction, or ask the VesnALIA Operator to coordinate a larger request.

## One conversation across VesnALIA

A compact voice panel lives above the sidebar footer and keeps the conversation connected as you navigate or collapse it. Microphone mute, hold-to-talk, output silence, and end-session controls work independently. Open the transcript for conversation details, focus selection, and links to threads that need visual review.

## Desktop and iPhone

The controls adapt to compact screens and iPhone safe areas. On a phone, moving VesnALIA to the background ends the voice connection; tap Start voice when you return. Your agents keep working.

## Setup

Add an OpenAI API key with GPT-Live access in this plugin's settings. The key stays on the VesnALIA server. Live voice and intent-model usage are billed to that OpenAI project; VesnALIA agents use their configured providers separately.

One session is available per VesnALIA installation. No audio is recorded. Text retention defaults to 30 days and can be disabled. Consequential actions require VesnALIA's visual approval surfaces.

This initial build has automated coverage and desktop/mobile browser checks. Real voice and completed cross-project delegation acceptance remain pending.
